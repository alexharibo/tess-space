01 / Mission Concept

ISR & Perimeter Reconnaissance

Observe. Patrol. Detect. Report.

Explore Mission
Capability— Field Proof— Discuss A Mission

Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance (ISR) operations depend on continuous situational awareness, particularly across large, remote or difficult-to-access areas. TESS SPACE envisions autonomous ground platforms supporting perimeter monitoring, reconnaissance and observation missions by carrying sensors into positions where maintaining a constant human presence may be impractical or unnecessarily demanding. Through autonomous navigation, onboard perception and mission planning, the UGV could support predefined patrol routes, observe designated areas and provide sensor information to operators for further assessment. The objective is to extend operational visibility, reduce repetitive monitoring tasks and help personnel focus on interpreting information and making mission-critical decisions. Human operators remain responsible for operational oversight and decisions, while the platform serves as a mobile sensor and reconnaissance asset.

02 / Mission Concept

Logistics & Medical Resupply

Transport. Resupply. Medical logistics.

Explore Mission
Capability— Field Proof— Discuss A Mission

Reliable logistics are essential to maintaining operational effectiveness, particularly when personnel are operating across difficult terrain or in environments where conventional transport is restricted. TESS SPACE explores how autonomous ground vehicles can support the movement of ammunition, water, batteries, medical equipment and other essential supplies between designated positions, reducing the physical burden on personnel and limiting the need for repeated manual transport. Using autonomous navigation and mission planning, a UGV could transport supplies along designated routes while adapting to changing terrain conditions within its operational limits. The same platform concept may also be configured to support medical logistics and casualty evacuation assistance, subject to appropriate equipment, safety validation and operational requirements. The goal is to provide flexible, unmanned transport capacity that allows personnel to dedicate more time and resources to their primary mission.

03 / Mission Concept

Squad Support / Follow

Follow. Alert. Assist. Protect.

Explore Mission
Capability— Field Proof— Discuss A Mission

Modern ground operations often require personnel to carry substantial amounts of equipment, including communication systems, batteries, sensors and mission-specific supplies. TESS SPACE is developing autonomous follow capabilities intended to allow an unmanned ground vehicle to accompany a designated person or moving unit, providing mobile carrying capacity without requiring continuous manual steering. By combining onboard perception, target tracking and motion control, the system is designed to maintain awareness of the designated leader while adjusting its movement to surrounding terrain and obstacles. Potential applications include equipment transport, mobile sensor support and resupply during extended movements. The concept aims to reduce the physical workload on personnel, improve operational flexibility and enable teams to move with additional equipment while maintaining human control over mission decisions.

04 / Mission Concept

Drone Deployment

Deploy. Extend reach. Aerial operations.

Explore Mission
Capability— Field Proof— Discuss A Mission

Unmanned aerial systems can provide valuable perspectives for reconnaissance, terrain assessment and situational awareness, but their operational reach is often constrained by battery capacity, launch locations and the need for supporting equipment. TESS SPACE explores the integration of drone deployment capabilities with autonomous ground platforms, creating a mobile support concept that could bring aerial systems closer to relevant mission areas. A suitably configured UGV could transport drones, supporting equipment and mission-specific payloads to designated deployment locations, reducing the need for personnel to carry these systems across difficult terrain. Depending on the integration and technical configuration, future solutions may also support launch, recovery and charging functions. By combining ground mobility with aerial observation capabilities, the concept aims to increase deployment flexibility and support coordinated unmanned operations while retaining appropriate human oversight.
